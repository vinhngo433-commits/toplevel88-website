Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I book?

Tap Reserve to send us your ritual, date and time on WhatsApp or Zalo — or call 0814 179 022. With 40 beds we can usually see you right away; booking ahead holds your preferred time.

How long does a ritual take?

Most rituals take between 60 and 110 minutes. Hot stone massage comes in 60, 90 and 110 minutes; combos run 90 or 110 minutes.

Do you have private rooms?

Yes. Alongside our 40 beds, we have private rooms for a quieter experience.

Is it easy to contact you from abroad?

Yes — our printed menus are in English, Chinese and Korean, and you can reach us on WhatsApp, Zalo, WeChat, KakaoTalk, LINE or Telegram.

Can I come with a group?

Yes. With 40 beds we can welcome friends, families and groups at the same time — send us a message beforehand so we can get everything ready.

Where are you and when are you open?

406 Nguyen Cong Tru, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City — about five minutes' walk from Ben Thanh Market. Open every day from 09:00 to 22:30.
